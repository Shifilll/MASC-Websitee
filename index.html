ABOUT MASC

More Than A Club,
We Are A Community.

Metro Mappilakkad Arts and Sports Club (MASC) is a community-driven club that brings people together through sports, arts, culture and social activities.

Our goal is to encourage talent, build strong relationships and create positive experiences within our community.
